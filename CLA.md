*Adapted from the Apache Software Foundation Individual CLA v2.2. Plain-language

## Why this exists (the short version)

Chickadee is AGPL-3.0. **Chickadee Cloud** — the optional hosted service that
funds it — runs the same brain core plus deployment glue we do not publish
(see [PROVENANCE.md](PROVENANCE.md)). Today that's unambiguously fine: one
author, sole copyright holder, and a license binds licensees, not the person
granting it.

The moment someone else's code lands here, that stops being automatic. Your
contribution would be licensed to us under AGPL like everyone else's — and if
our hosted service then ran a modified build containing it, §13 would oblige
us to publish that modified service. We'd rather ask you up front for a
license broad enough to keep the project's funding model working than merge
your patch and discover we can't ship it.

So: **you keep your copyright.** You grant us a license to use your
contribution — including in Chickadee Cloud and in Dashie, our closed
commercial product — and you also license it to everyone else under AGPL-3.0,
the same as the rest of the repo. You are not assigning ownership and you are
not signing away your own right to use your own code however you like.

If that trade isn't one you want to make, that's a legitimate choice — open an
issue instead of a pull request and describe the change. We'd rather have the
bug report than nothing.

## How to sign

Add a line to [CONTRIBUTORS.md](CONTRIBUTORS.md) in your first pull request:

```
- Your Name <your@email> — GitHub @yourhandle — agreed to CLA.md on YYYY-MM-DD
```

Adding that line, in a commit authored by you, is your signature. One
signature covers all your future contributions to this project.
